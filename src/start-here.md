# Start Here

New to Freq51? Follow these steps to get a node connected without having to read every guide first.

## Your First 30 Minutes

1. Get a [Meshtastic-compatible device](https://meshtastic.org/docs/hardware/devices/).
2. Flash the latest Meshtastic **Alpha** version that supports your device. Alpha may be less stable than Beta, but it is reliable for normal use. See the [Firmware Update Guide](advanced-configuration/firmware-updates.md) for step-by-step help.
3. Pair the Meshtastic app with your node over Bluetooth, USB, or Wi-Fi.
4. Apply the community settings with the [Freq51 configuration tool](https://tools.freq51.net/config/).
5. Send a short test message on the Freq51 mesh.
6. Check [MeshView](https://meshview.freq51.net/) to see nearby infrastructure and, if your node opts in, its map presence.
7. Join [Freq51 Discord](https://discord.gg/qmeeRPkq3g) if you need help.

## Before You Deploy Anything Permanent

- Start with the `CLIENT` role unless the community has helped you choose another role.
- Test from the intended location before mounting equipment permanently.
- Do not deploy a `ROUTER` without coordination; a poorly placed router can reduce mesh performance.
