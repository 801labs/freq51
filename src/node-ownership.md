# Node Ownership and Claiming

Claiming a node connects it to your Discord account for Weekly Net check-ins, streaks, and support. It does not change the radio configuration or transfer control of another person’s equipment.

## Claim Your Node

1. Join the [Freq51 Discord](https://discord.gg/qmeeRPkq3g).
2. Use the `/claimnode` command.
3. Follow the bot’s prompts to select and verify your node.
4. Use `/usernodes` to confirm the nodes associated with your account.

## If Something Does Not Look Right

- Do not claim a node you do not own or operate.
- If a node is already assigned, ask for help in Discord rather than repeatedly trying to claim it.
- Include the node’s long name, short name, or node ID when requesting help.

After claiming a node, see [Weekly Net](weeklynet.md) for check-in details and [Getting Help](support.md) for a support template.
